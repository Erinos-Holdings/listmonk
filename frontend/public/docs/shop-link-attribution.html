Shop Link Attribution

Every subscriber carries one shop link per brand, and it decides who gets credit for their purchases. Every brand with its own website goes on the standard below at onboarding, before its first sign-up.

Robbie Jackson · 2026-11-03 · A static copy of the working document, reshaped as a page 2026-11-08. Counts are point-in-time.

Related: Brands › Usage (the journey guide and the Lifecycle series decisions) · Interval Evidence Brief (the case for the journey intervals) · AI-generated Journey Campaigns (the copy direction; every link in a step follows this standard)

Summary

What it is

For each brand, a subscriber holds one complete link, such as https://bnkdwellness.com/?ref=arielctyson. Every email and SMS uses that link exactly as stored.

Who it credits

The ?ref= value is the creator who gets credit. A link with no ?ref= credits nobody.

Where it lives

listmonk is the one place the link is stored. Clerk SMS copies it into the brand's <Brand> Shop Link contact field.

The rule

At onboarding, each brand gets three settings: its website, its founder's referral slug, and its official launch time. Without them, referrals from the brand's own site are lost. That is what happened to BNKD from July to November 2026.

The four kinds of link

Every stored link is one of four kinds. The kind records why the link is what it is, and its rank decides whether a later sign-up can replace it.

KindRankWho gets itThe link
Self2 highestA curated creator who has their own siteBrand site ?ref= their own slug
Creator1Arrived through a creator's page or a creator's ?ref= linkBrand site ?ref= that creator
Founder0Signed up with no referral before the launchBrand site ?ref= the founder
Corporate0Signed up with no referral after the launchThe plain brand site, no ?ref=

A higher rank always wins. Founder and corporate share the lowest rank, and between those two the newer sign-up wins.

A sign-up through the founder's own referral link is a creator link. It has the same URL as a founder grant, but one rank higher.

Two sites, two roles. The brand's own site (for example, ruzepouches.com) is the official site, and every stored link points to it. The brand's curatedfor.you store is the outlet where discounts live. A campaign may link to either one, but the person's stored link does not change.

How a sign-up is classified

Each sign-up runs through these checks in order, and the first one that applies decides the link.

  1. The person is a curated creator with their own site→ self, ?ref= their own slug. Recognised when they join, and again whenever their creator record changes.
  2. They ticked the "keep me updated" box at checkout, and the order credits a creator→ creator, ?ref= the order's creator (settled with Chris, Nov 3, 2026). The brand's own name is not a creator; the founder is.
  3. They came through a creator's page(curatedfor.you/<creator>/… or komigo.me/<creator>/…) → creator, ?ref= that creator. The brand's own pages (curatedfor.you/<brand>, /b/<brand>) are not creator pages.
  4. They came to the brand site with a valid ?ref=→ creator, ?ref= that value.
  5. Anything else(no referral, the brand page, a checkout with no creator on the order) → founder if the sign-up is before the launch, corporate if it is at or after the launch.

The time of the sign-up itself decides before or after the launch, not today's date.

Before and after a brand's launch

The launch changes only how new sign-ups with no referral are credited. Nothing already stored is rewritten at the launch.

PhaseWhenA sign-up with no referral getsReferred sign-ups and creators

Pre-launchSettings in place, before the launch timeFounder: ?ref=<founder>Creator or self, as usual
Launch momentThe exact launch timeNothing is rewritten; founder grants made so far stayNo change
Post-launchAfter the launch timeCorporate: the plain brand siteCreator or self, as usual
  • A founder grant is not permanent. A later referral (creator, rank 1) or the person becoming a creator (self, rank 2) replaces it. A later sign-up with no referral after the launch also ends it, replacing it with corporate. This is the settled rule (agreed with Chris, Oct 16, 2026).
  • The launch time is an exact instant in Mountain time. BNKD's is 7:00 AM MST, Nov 5, 2026. Daylight saving time ended Nov 1, so that is 14:00 UTC.
  • Moving the launch is one settings update, and it only affects sign-ups that land between the old and the new time.

What changes a stored link

Only a new sign-up, a change in creator status, or the sweep changes a stored link. Clicks, sends and purchases never take credit away from anyone.

A new sign-up or opt-inReplaces the link only if it ranks equal or higher; at equal rank the newer one wins. After launch, a sign-up with no referral ends a founder grant

A purchase without ticking the boxNever changes anything

The creator resignsSelf is removed and rebuilt from their sign-up
A first purchase by a subscriber with no link at all (rare)Fills one in: founder before the launch, corporate after

The launch time passingNever

Email and SMS

listmonk holds the link. Email and SMS both read it from there and never build their own.

  • Email (listmonk). Insert the shop link with the editor's attribute picker. For a product page, paste the product URL and use Personalize product link, which adds the subscriber's ?ref= and strips any ref already on the pasted URL. Never type ?ref= by hand: a creator's own ?ref= copied from a browser would credit them for everyone.
  • SMS (Clerk). Each SMS opt-in copies the person's link into the brand's <Brand> Shop Link contact field (RUZE Shop Link, Thirsty Girl Shop Link, BNKD Shop Link). When a person's link changes later, Clerk is updated straight away, and a daily check catches anything missed within 24 hours. SMS reaches US and Canadian (+1) numbers only (decided with Daryl, Nov 4, 2026): an international sign-up keeps its email link and consent record but is never sent to Clerk, and the 31 international contacts that were in Clerk were removed that day. Filter a brand's contacts on Brands contains <Brand>.
  • A click is tracked, and Campaign Analytics shows shop-link clicks by kind (Self, Creator, Founder, Corporate).

Onboarding checklist for every new brand

All of these happen before the brand's first campaign or SMS send, and ideally before its first sign-up.

StepWhatOwner
1Confirm the brand's site address, the founder's referral slug (the ref the brand's own links carry) and the official launch date and time in Mountain timeBrand team + Marketing
2Set the three settings on the brand (site, founder, launch)Engineering
3If the brand already has subscribers: preview the sweep with the new settings, then run itEngineering
4SMS: confirm the sign-up form puts the phone field behind an unchecked SMS consent checkbox that names the brandMarketing
5SMS: create the brand's option on the Brands field and a <Brand> Shop Link text field in Clerk, and set the field on one test contactDaryl
6SMS: connect the brand and the field to the Clerk sync, deploy, and backfill the earlier SMS opt-insEngineering
7Campaigns link through the attribute picker or Personalize product link, never a hand-typed ?ref=Marketing

A brand with no site of its own (only a curatedfor.you page) skips steps 1–3. Its sign-ups arrive through creator pages, which the old rule credits correctly.

Keeping links current

Since November 4, 2026 attribution updates in real time, so nothing needs to run before a campaign or SMS send.

  • Email: a creator is recognised the moment they join a list, and their links change as soon as their creator record does.
  • SMS: Clerk is updated when a link changes, and a daily check at 14:00 UTC catches anything missed.
  • The sweep and the Clerk repair are now repair tools. Engineering runs them after event replays, after a brand's settings change, or when something looks off. One routine use remains: after a purchase-list catch-up on a brand.

Worked example: BNKD

BNKD collected sign-ups from July 23 to November 3, 2026 without its three settings, so every brand-site referral was dropped. All of it was recovered on November 3.

Changed 2026-11-09Unsubscribe now means the brand, not the list. Someone who unsubscribes from any of a brand's emails, or unticks the brand on Manage Preferences, comes off every marketing list of that brand at once — the newsletter, the journey step lists, the first-order and SKU trigger lists, the re-engagement queues and any imported segment — with other brands untouched and transactional lists exempt. For the counts below that means an unsubscribed member is now unsubscribed on every one of the brand's lists rather than on the one they clicked from, so a list's unsubscribed share will look larger than it did; the brand's subscriber attribution itself is unchanged, because a stored shop link is never removed by an unsubscribe. The same rule was applied backwards to each brand's history.

These counts are the whole list at the time of the sweep, unsubscribed members included. A campaign reaches fewer: campaign 259's 1,198 recipients hold 829 creator, 305 founder, 62 self and 2 with no link. Clerk counts only people who gave a phone (654 BNKD contacts).

Settings now in place: site https://bnkdwellness.com, founder arielctyson, launch 7:00 AM MST Nov 5, 2026.

On SMS, BNKD opt-ins now reach Clerk with Brands = BNKD and the BNKD Shop Link filled in. The earlier SMS opt-ins were backfilled on Nov 3: 654 contacts now carry the brand, and UK and Australian numbers were repaired on Nov 3 as well.

The recovery was possible only because each subscriber's first sign-up page is kept. Setting the brand up at onboarding avoids the gap entirely: every email sent in those three months carried a link that credited nobody.

Open questions

One point is still undecided. Checkout opt-ins (settled Nov 3) and the every-brand Clerk repair (built Nov 4) are resolved and folded into the sections above.

Does anyone read "Corporate" as "came from the brand's own site"?

In these rules corporate means only that nobody is credited, and it can come from a sign-up on either site. Marketing should confirm that no report treats the Corporate bar in Shop Link Attribution as brand-site traffic.
